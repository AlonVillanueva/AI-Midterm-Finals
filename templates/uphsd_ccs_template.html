IV. Institutional Grading Criteria

  • Quizzes / Formative Assessments: 25%
  • Problem Sets & Case Studies: 25%
  • Major Examination (Midterm / Final): 50%
  • Laboratory Exercises / Machine Problems: 50%
  • Practical Examination: 30%
  • Laboratory Behavior & Ethics: 20%
Final Grade = (Lecture Component × 0.50) + (Laboratory Component × 0.50) | Passing Grade: 75.0% (3.00)
V. Course Policies & Academic Guidelines

1. Academic Integrity & Ethics: Students are strictly expected to submit their own original code and solutions. Plagiarism, uncredited reproduction of AI-generated artifacts, and unauthorized code sharing constitute major academic offenses under the Institutional Student Handbook.

2. Laboratory Etiquette: Food and drinks are strictly forbidden inside the CCS laboratories. Computers must be properly shut down and workstations cleaned after every laboratory session.

3. Late Submissions: Machine problems submitted beyond the scheduled deadline are subject to a 10% daily grade deduction unless accompanied by a verified medical certificate.
